Camera Eye Tracker

See where you look on this screen with nothing but the normal camera. Three figures light up only while you look at them. The camera picture is never shown and never leaves this device.

  1. Set up. Face in view, light on your face, device held still.
  2. Calibrate. Follow 9 dots with your eyes (about 20 seconds), then 4 test dots that measure the error.
  3. Look. The figure you look at lights up, and a dot shows where your eyes point. Tap where you are looking to correct it.

The camera can sit anywhere: above, below or beside the screen, or as an external webcam. Calibration learns where it is. Keep it in the same place afterwards.

Expect an error of about 2 to 3 cm on a phone (best case about 1.5 cm). Glasses, poor light and head movement make it worse. That is enough to tell which part of the screen you look at, not which word you read. After calibration the app measures your own error and shows it.
